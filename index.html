04The Human

ABOUT
ME

As a Web Developer specializing in CMS, what I really care about is the end result, how the site feels, not just how it works. I believe the user should genuinely enjoy what they're interacting with, visually pleased, not just functionally served.

I always try to deliver work I'd be comfortable putting my name on, not just work that technically checks the box. I refuse to treat my work as a task list, if I see a better way to do it, I'll say so.

What excites me most is projects with a personality. Something that doesn't just follow the market, but has its own identity, even if that identity is playful or a little unpolished by "professional" standards. Personality speaks louder than perfection, and I'd rather build something people remember than something that just blends in.
