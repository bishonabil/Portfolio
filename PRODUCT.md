# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript — no framework, no build step, deploy anywhere.

## Users

Primary: Recruiters and hiring managers at tech companies, and freelance clients (startups, agencies, businesses needing a developer). Both audiences land with high intent — they are evaluating whether Bishoy is worth reaching out to.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Bishoy Nabil, a web developer specializing in CMS development with full-stack experience. The site's job is to convert a skeptical visitor into someone who sends a message or books a call, within a single page.

## Positioning

Bishoy occupies the intersection of CMS specialist and full-stack practitioner — someone who can own the entire delivery chain, from CMS architecture through front-of-front-end craft. This dual fluency is not common and is the portfolio's core claim.

## Operating Context

Visited on desktop and mobile, typically from a LinkedIn link, a GitHub profile, or a direct referral. Visitors spend 30–90 seconds deciding. The portfolio must land a clear identity, show real project work, and make contact feel low-friction.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: Hero, Projects, Skills, About, Contact
- Projects: placeholder cards built from Bishoy's descriptions — no live URLs or screenshots yet; structure ready for future real assets
- No backend — plain static site
- Must be responsive across desktop and mobile

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Bishoy Nabil
- Visual world: Neo-brutalism — hard offset shadows, bold strokes, raw grid, high contrast; dark ground with saturated, clashing color accents
- Voice: direct, confident, no fluff; speaks to peers and clients alike

## Evidence on Hand

- Developer's own description of specialization: CMS dev + full-stack projects
- No real project screenshots available yet — placeholder cards required
- No testimonials available — do not fabricate

## Product Principles

1. **Identity before decoration.** The first viewport names Bishoy and his claim. No visitor should be uncertain what they're looking at after one second.
2. **Proof, not performance.** Projects lead with what was built and why it matters, not technology logos.
3. **Low-friction contact.** Contact is always one click away; the form is visible, not buried.
4. **Committed world.** The neo-brutalist visual language is executed fully — not softened to placate taste; the form earns trust through commitment.
5. **Mobile-first, desktop-celebrated.** Works on a phone; rewards a laptop.
